Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Mori ("we", "our", or "us") is a recipe discovery app built by an independent developer. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We keep this simple because we genuinely don't collect more than we need.

1. What we collect

  • Email address — when you join the waitlist or create an account.
  • Onboarding preferences — dietary goals, cuisine preferences, skill level, budget, and ingredient dislikes. These power your personalised recipe deck.
  • Swipe and interaction data — which recipes you swipe right or left on, save, add to your grocery list, or mark as cooked. This is used solely to improve your recommendations.
  • Pantry items — ingredients you add manually or select during onboarding.
  • Meal plans and grocery lists — saved to your account so they persist across sessions.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name unless you provide it in your profile.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. AI features and Anthropic

Mori uses Claude (made by Anthropic) for three features:

  • Taste profile — your swipe history is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a short personalised paragraph describing your cooking style. This is cached in our database and not re-sent on every session.
  • Macro estimates — a recipe's ingredient list is sent to Anthropic's API to estimate calories, protein, carbs, and fat. Results are stored and shared across all users for that recipe.
  • Storage tips — after you mark a recipe as cooked, the recipe title is sent to Anthropic's API to generate storage and usage suggestions.

Data sent to Anthropic is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy. We do not send your name, email, or account details to Anthropic.

4. Instacart

When you tap "Order on Instacart", your grocery list (a list of ingredient names) is sent to Instacart to pre-populate a cart. You complete checkout inside the Instacart app. Instacart's own privacy policy governs that interaction. Mori receives an affiliate commission on attributed orders through Impact.

5. Data storage

Your data is stored on Supabase, a hosted PostgreSQL provider. Data is stored in the US. Row-level security ensures you can only read and write your own data.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your account data is retained until you delete your account. To delete your account and all associated data, email hello@getmori.app with the subject "Delete my account". We will process the request within 7 days.

7. Children

Mori is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify users via email if the changes affect how we use personal data.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at hello@getmori.app.
